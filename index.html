My Final Year Dissertation

One of my biggest university projects was developing a cognitive rehabilitation game using Unreal Engine and C++.

The project explored how video games could potentially support people recovering from strokes through interactive cognitive exercises.

I developed three mini-games:

  • Memory: Exercises focused on remembering information.
  • Reaction: Challenges involving response times and reflexes.
  • Pattern Recognition: Activities focused on identifying and remembering patterns.

The project also included score tracking and supporting systems to connect the different mini-games.

This was particularly rewarding because it allowed me to combine programming and game development with a project that had a potential real-world application.
