What Is Pokémon?

Pokémon began in 1996 as a pair of role-playing games created by Game Freak and published by Nintendo. The name is short for “Pocket Monsters,” and the series is based around catching, training and battling fictional creatures.

The franchise has grown far beyond video games. It now includes trading cards, animated shows and films, toys, mobile games and competitive esports. The series has always encouraged players to choose a team, explore new regions and build a close bond with their Pokémon.

One of my all-time favourites is Totodile, the cheerful Big Jaw Pokémon from the Johto region. It is a Water type known for its powerful jaws and energetic personality.
